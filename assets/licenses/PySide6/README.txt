PySide6 (Qt for Python) 6.12.0, with shiboken6 6.12.0 and Qt 6.12.0 (from the PySide6-Essentials and
shiboken6 packages on PyPI), draw the merger's window. They are used, unmodified, under the GNU Lesser General
Public License version 3 (LGPL-3.0-only.txt), which builds on the GNU General Public License version 3
(GPL-3.0-only.txt). Copyright (C) The Qt Company Ltd. and other contributors.

Qt's DLLs (qt6*.dll), PySide6's and shiboken6's (pyside6.abi3.dll, shiboken6.abi3.dll) and their modules
(PySide6\*.pyd, shiboken6\*.pyd) are separate files in the tools' folder, and Qt's plugins are in
PySide6\qt-plugins\, so you can replace them with modified versions.

Source code:
  Qt 6.12.0: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/6.12/6.12.0/single/
  PySide6 and shiboken6 6.12.0: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/pyside6/PySide6-6.12.0-src/
  (also https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/tag/?h=v6.12.0)
